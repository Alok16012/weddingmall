Privacy Policy — WeddingMall.Online

Applies to the Android app WeddingMall.Online (package online.weddingmall.app). Effective 6 August 2026.

The short version

You can browse venues and vendors, shortlist them and create a matrimonial biodata without an account. The only information the app sends anywhere is what you type into an enquiry form, and that goes to the vendor you chose to contact. Your shortlist, your selected city and everything in the biodata maker stay on your phone.

What the app collects

InformationWhenWhy
Your name and mobile number, and an optional wedding date Only when you send an enquiry to a vendor So the vendor can call you back about your event
Mobile number Only if you choose guest verification To send a one-time code confirming the number is yours
Email address and password Only for vendors signing in to a business account To authenticate the vendor and show that vendor its own enquiries

Nothing else is collected. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracker in the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your shortlist and your selected city.
  • Everything in the biodata maker — the details you enter and any photo you choose or capture. The biodata PDF is generated on your phone. The photo is never uploaded to us.

You can erase all of it from More → Privacy & Data → Clear on-device data, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load vendor listings and to show the offline screen.
  • Camera — only if you tap “take a photo” in the biodata maker. The photo is used on-device to render your biodata and is not sent to us.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older) — to save a biodata PDF you download.

The app does not request location access.

Who we share it with

  • The vendor you contact. An enquiry is delivered to that vendor and to no one else. We do not sell or rent enquiry details.
  • Supabase, our hosting and database provider, which stores enquiries and vendor accounts on our behalf. Traffic to it is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

How long it is kept

Enquiries are retained while they are useful to the vendor for follow-up. Vendor accounts are kept until the vendor asks for closure. On-device data lasts until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Browse without giving any information at all — no account is required.
  • Clear all on-device data from within the app at any time.
  • Ask us to delete an enquiry record or a vendor account by writing to the address below. We will confirm your request and act on it.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning a wedding. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
